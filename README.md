# Living City

A design for an open-world city that remembers.

Open-world crowds are extras: created near the player, given a few minutes of behaviour, deleted when the player looks away.
Follow one for hours and the illusion breaks, because nobody has a home to go back to.
Kill one and the world forgets as soon as the body is cleared.

This project designs the opposite: every person on the street is a resident with a home, a job, a household and a day that ends somewhere, and what the player does to them is still felt a hundred hours later, by specific people and by whole districts.

- [docs/design.md](docs/design.md): the design, including prior art, the seven layers, budget and milestones.

Status: design only.
The first milestone is a game-free prototype on a real city's street map.
